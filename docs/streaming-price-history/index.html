How much have streaming prices gone up since launch?

Eight plans in this list have a sourced launch price. Most cost more today than at launch. Apple TV+ went from $4.99 a month in 2019 to $14.99. Spotify Premium went from $9.99 in 2011 to $12.99. Xbox Game Pass Essential is still $9.99, the same as the one plan at launch in 2017. Every figure links its source.

Today's prices were checked Nov 25, 2026, for new US subscribers, before tax. Intro offers and student prices are left out. A launch figure is shown only when this list has a sourced one. Some launches were a single plan, so that figure is not a like-for-like match with every plan sold today.
